Income Tax Inspector Salary Progression

Projection for joining as Income Tax Inspector in 2028 at age 26, Chennai posting, with two separate fitment cases: 1.80x and 2.20x. HRA is shown separately.

Assumptions

Joining
2028, age 26
City / HRA
Chennai, X-city HRA
HRA rates used
24%, 27%, 30%
DA increase
2.5% every 6 months
Annual increment
3% basic per year
Pay Commissions
8th: 2026, 9th: 2036, 10th: 2046, 11th: 2056
Promotion path
ITI → ITO → ACIT → DCIT → JCIT
PF / EPF
No separate PF; NPS is used
Gross formula
Gross / mo = Basic + DA + HRA + Transport Allowance
In-hand formula
In-hand / mo = Gross - Employee NPS - Income Tax

Employee NPS is deducted from your salary. Government NPS is not deducted from your salary; it is shown separately only for CTC calculation. CGHS, CGEGIS, and Professional Tax are removed.

Case A — 1.80x fitment factor at every Pay Commission

Case B — 2.20x fitment factor at every Pay Commission

Note: The 2.20x case becomes very large by retirement because the same high fitment is repeated in 2036, 2046, and 2056. Treat it as an aggressive upper-case projection.

Column division

Earnings columns
Basic, DA, HRA, Transport, Gross
Salary deductions
Employee NPS and Income Tax
Final salary
In-hand / mo
CTC columns
Gross/year, Govt NPS/year, Total CTC/year

Amounts are future nominal rupees. Gross salary/year = Gross/month × 12. Total CTC/year = Gross salary/year + Government NPS/year.
